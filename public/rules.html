GoldenNotes Community Rules

GoldenNotes is for sharing notes, learning resources, and helping other students. These rules apply to accounts, uploads, study guides, comments, and other community features.

Moderation decisions should consider context, intent, repetition, severity, and the impact on other users. Staff should apply rules consistently and explain actions where practical.

1. Respect other people

  • Be respectful, even when you disagree. Criticise ideas, not a person's identity or personal traits.
  • No bullying, targeted harassment, threats, hate speech, or encouraging others to pile onto someone.
  • Do not impersonate another student, teacher, moderator, or administrator.
  • Do not post private information about yourself or someone else without permission.
  • Do not spam, flood pages, evade moderation, or deliberately disrupt the service.

2. Notes, documents, and study guides

  • Upload material that is relevant to learning and that you have permission to share.
  • Use clear, accurate titles and descriptions. Do not disguise harmful or unrelated files as study material.
  • Do not upload malware, scams, explicit content, stolen private documents, or material intended to harm users.
  • Respect copyright. Do not claim another person's work as your own; provide attribution where appropriate.
  • Staff may remove content that breaks these rules or creates a safety, privacy, or legal risk.

3. Swearing and profanity

Profanity is judged in context. A casual exclamation is not the same as using a slur or swearing at a person to intimidate or humiliate them.

  • Usually okay: occasional mild profanity that is not aimed at anyone and does not derail a learning space.
  • Please avoid: repeated strong profanity, disruptive profanity in study material, or language that makes a shared space hostile.
  • Not allowed: slurs, hateful language, targeted abusive messages, threats, or using profanity to harass someone.

Automated profanity detection can make mistakes, including with quotations, names, and educational discussion. A detection count is a moderation signal, not proof of misconduct by itself.

4. Do's and don'ts

  • Do: share useful resources, credit creators, protect account credentials, report problems, and follow staff instructions that are consistent with these rules.
  • Do: explain context calmly if a post is misunderstood, and use the appeal route if you believe a moderation action was wrong.
  • Don't: threaten people, post personal information, spam, exploit bugs, bypass a ban, or misuse report tools.
  • Don't: upload content you do not have permission to share or use the platform to cheat, scam, or target others.

5. What happens when a rule is broken?

Actions depend on the seriousness of the incident and the user's history. Not every incident receives every step, and severe incidents may skip directly to stronger action.

  1. Reminder or warning: for a minor or first-time issue, staff may explain the rule and ask for a change.
  2. Content removal: a violating upload or post may be removed.
  3. Temporary restriction or ban: repeated disruption, harassment, or a more serious violation may lead to a time-limited restriction. The reason and duration should be recorded.
  4. Longer restriction: serious threats, targeted hate, repeated ban evasion, or ongoing harmful behaviour may justify a longer ban or account review.

There is no automatic fixed punishment for a particular swear word. Staff should consider context, severity, repetition, impact, and relevant prior moderation history. A profanity counter alone should not trigger a punishment without review.

6. Moderator and administrator expectations

  • Use the least restrictive action that reasonably addresses the issue, while prioritising user safety.
  • Give a clear reason for bans and content removals. Do not use staff powers to settle personal disputes.
  • Protect user information and only access account data needed for moderation.
  • Do not promise confidentiality that the service cannot guarantee. Escalate threats, safety concerns, and uncertain cases to an administrator.

7. Appeals and reporting

If you think content was removed or your account was restricted by mistake, contact the moderation team through the official support/contact route available on GoldenNotes. Include your username, the action you are appealing, and a short explanation. Do not post private account tokens or passwords.

Reports should be made in good faith. Deliberately false or spam reports may themselves be moderated.

8. Updates

These rules may be updated as GoldenNotes grows. Important changes should be announced clearly. If a situation is not covered here, staff should use consistent judgement, explain the decision, and prioritise safety, fairness, privacy, and learning.

9. Reporting to school officials

In any case of misconduct declared in the PSHS Code of Conduct
which may count as an infraction shall be reported to school authorities, the following are reasons:

  1. Engaging in any form of bullying or harassing behaviors toward others
  2. Engaging in dishonest behavior such as Academic Dishonesty
  3. Engaging in physical altercations or causing harm to others
  4. Engaging in sexual misconduct or inappropriate behaviors
  5. Participating in any form of gambling
  6. Using or threatening violence against others
