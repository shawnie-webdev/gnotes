1 - The Corkboard

The Corkboard is a way to submit schedules, or assignments, or announcements. Keep it cool.

To use the Corkboard

  1. Go to Corkboard.

  2. Enter your room ID, if you want to join your class, you must follow the format:

    [First 4 letters of your section in uppercase][Batch number] -- For example:

    For someone in Section 7 - Gold and has a Batch Number of 2032: GOLD32

  3. Now, enter your subject, you can use main if you want.
  4. Then, press join.
